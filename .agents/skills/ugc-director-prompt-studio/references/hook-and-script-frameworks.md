# Indonesian UGC Hook, Script & Retention Beat Frameworks

Use these frameworks as the **Scriptwriter Specialist & UGC Creator** layer before writing visual prompts.

---

## 1. Emotional Added-Value Formula (Emosi -> Painful -> Solusi -> Before vs After -> CTA)

Generic affiliates fail because they only read product titles or list dry specs. Outperform competitors by grounding every **Narrative Storyboard** (including single-clip 15s videos) in this 5-part conversion arc:

| Narrative Stage | Conversion Job | Indonesian UGC Copy Pattern (Natural & Compliant) | Visual & Safety Guardrail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Sentuh Emosi & Painful Nyata** | Trigger immediate *"Itu aku banget!"* recognition in seconds 1–3. | *"Jujur paling males kalau lagi kerja/jalan malah pegel atau barang berantakan gara-gara pakai yang gampang melorot."* | Express relatable human emotion via `@me` facial expression/body language; never depict `@ProductAuthority` broken. |
| **2. Apa yang Di-Solve (Added Value)** | Prove specifically **how** and **why** this item fixes the pain better than generic alternatives. | *"Makanya langsung ganti ke ini — penguncinya presisi dan bahannya tebal rapi jadi langsung beres sekali pasang."* | Pair with concrete spec/material facts and macro detail when zoomed in. |
| **3. Before vs After** | Contrast everyday life *Before* (ribet, berantakan, pegel) vs *After* (rapi, nyaman, tenang). | *"Dulu sebelum pakai ini rasanya ribet banget; sekarang begitu dipakai seharian tetap terasa nyaman dan meja jauh lebih lega."* | Keep contrast grounded in daily comfort/organization—never write banned medical claims or show torn/defective `@ProductAuthority`. |
| **4. Objection Killer** | Neutralize the #1 hesitation buyers type in comments. | *"Buat yang takut gampang goyang atau sempit, bobot dan ukurannya ternyata aman banget buat harian."* | Reinforced again in `pinned_comment_faq_id`. |
| **5. CTA Persuasif** | Turn emotional clarity into immediate basket taps. | *"Mumpung stoknya masih lengkap, langsung cek di Keranjang Oranye / cek keranjang di bawah sekarang."* | Strictly follow Shopee / TikTok / Dual CTA rules. |

## 2. Six Indonesian Scroll-Stopper Hook Archetypes (First 3 Seconds)

Avoid generic TV-commercial openers (*"Halo guys, kali ini aku mau spill..."*, *"Apakah kalian pernah mengalami..."*). Start mid-thought or proof-first:

| Archetype | When to Use | Spoken Indonesian Pattern | Visual Pairing (Scene 1) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Pattern Interrupt** | High-competition categories (home gear, beauty, bags). | *"Jujur baru ngeh benda sekecil ini nyelametin meja kerjaku setiap hari."* | Medium close-up with crisp eye-level presence + `@ProductAuthority` foregrounded. |
| **2. Relatable Micro-Pain** | Ergonomic, storage, kitchen, or commuter items. | *"Sering pegel atau ribet pas beresin barang? Ini alasan aku langsung ganti model ini."* | Natural Indonesian room setting, calm relatable expression holding/beside `@ProductAuthority`. |
| **3. Contrarian Myth-Bust** | Products buyers think are flimsy or overpriced. | *"Awalnya kupikir ini cuma gimmick tipis, ternyata pas dipegang langsung berasa kokohnya."* | Clean 3/4 product reveal on warm wooden surface, confident push-in. |
| **4. Proof-First Result** | Skincare texture, stain/lint removers, lighting, compact gear. | *"Lihat bedanya pemakaian rutin seminggu: tampilannya jauh lebih rapi tanpa effort ribet."* | Bright window-lit close-up framing `@ProductAuthority` beside clean result context. |
| **5. Value/Logic Breakdown** | Mid-ticket hero products needing justification. | *"Daripada beli dua kali gara-gara gampang melorot, mending cek detail penguncinya ini dulu."* | Clean tabletop medium shot highlighting craftsmanship without damaging product. |
| **6. Specific Buyer Call-Out** | Niche targeting (anak kos, WFH, ibu muda, traveler). | *"Anak kos atau tim WFH yang mejanya sempit, wajib lihat ukuran ringkasnya ini."* | Compact tidy Indonesian desk/corner with `@ProductAuthority` naturally scaled. |

## 3. Retention Beat-Sheet Templates (3, 5, and 7 Scenes)

Keep `vo_script_id` strictly within **12–16 spoken Indonesian words per scene** (~3–4.5s natural pace) and `on_screen_headline_id` at **<= 5 words**. Together, Scenes 1–3 form a seamless **15-second continuous narrative**.

### A. 3-Scene Sprint (~15s Utuh — Default 1× Copy-Paste Arc)
| Scene | Narrative Job | Shot Grammar | Flow Action | VO Goal (12–16 words ID) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Emosi & Painful Nyata** | Medium Close-Up + Gentle Push-In | `New Scene` | Sentuh rasa lelah/ribet harian yang langsung relate dengan pembeli. |
| **2** | **Apa yang Di-Solve (Solusi & Bukti)** | Slow Macro Pan / Clean Demo | `Jump to` | Jelaskan mekanisme/bahan konkret yang menyelesaikan masalah tadi. |
| **3** | **Before vs After + Objection + CTA** | Hero 3/4 Subtle Dolly-In | `Jump to` | Kontras Before-After singkat + jawab keraguan + CTA platform. |

### B. 5-Scene Conversion Standard (~25–30s — Multi-Clip Arc)
| Scene | Narrative Job | Shot Grammar | Flow Action | VO Goal (12–16 words ID) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Hook Emosi & Painful** | Medium Close-Up / Hero Push-In | `New Scene` | Pemicu emosi + *painful* harian agar berhenti *scroll*. |
| **2** | **Situasi Before (Frustrasi Harian)** | Natural Room Framing | `Extend` | Gambarkan repotnya kondisi *before* secara jujur tanpa merusak produk. |

## 4. Spoken Indonesian Persona Matrix & Comment Funneling

| Persona Style | Spoken Rhythm & Word Choice | Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Teman Jujur** | *"Jujur ya...", "Menurutku paling kerasa itu...", "Buat harian aman banget."* | Over-hyped exclamation spam, robotic formal Indonesian (*"Produk ini merupakan..."*). |
| **Racun Antusias** | *"Serius ini kepake banget!", "Detailnya rapi pol...", "Mumpung stoknya masih aman..."* | Fake urgency lies, shouting tone, spammy superlatives. |
| **Reviewer Elegan** | *"Dari segi finishing...", "Proporsinya pas di ruang...", "Kenyamanan jangka panjangnya terasa."* | Slang overload, rushed phrasing. |

**Pinned Comment FAQ Strategy:** End your script flow by pairing the blueprint with one honest `pinned_comment_faq_id` answering dimensions, care/washing, variant advice, or battery/charging specs in 1–2 sentences so buyers convert directly in comments.

## 5. Platform CTA, Anti-Shadowban & Speech Orthography Guardrails

- **Shopee Video:** Say `Keranjang Oranye` or `Tautan Produk di bawah` (never say `Keranjang Kuning`).
- **TikTok Shop:** Say `Keranjang Kuning`.
- **Dual Mode (`BOTH`):** Say neutral `cek keranjang di bawah` in the VO so one video edit works cleanly across both platforms, while separating `shopee_caption_and_hashtags` and `tiktok_caption_and_hashtags`.
- **Anti-Shadowban First 3 Seconds:** Never open Scene 1 with raw hard-selling triggers (`Beli sekarang`, `Diskon besar`, `Murah banget`, `WA`, `No HP`). Lead with emotional pain or real solution in Scene 1 and keep CTAs at the end.
- **Clean Indonesian Speech Orthography & Audio Isolation:** Spell Indonesian script words completely—never write SMS abbreviations (`yg`, `bgt`, `tdk`, `dgn`, `utk`) that make Veo 3.1 / Omni Flash stumble. Target **11–14 words/scene** (~33–40 words across 3 scenes for clean 15s delivery) with `medium close-up`, `natural native Indonesian voice and accent`, and `no background music` so lip-sync stays crisp.
- **Banned Claims (Zero Tolerance):** Never write absolute medical promises (`menyembuhkan`, `mengobati`, `sembuh total`, `mencegah penyakit`, `100% ampuh`, `hilang permanen`, `putih dalam X hari`), deceptive pricing (`termurah se-Indonesia`, `pasti gratis`), or off-platform redirects (`beli via WA/DM/IG`). Always frame benefits softly (`membantu merawat`, `terasa lebih nyaman`, `cek promo di keranjang`).
- **Visual Integrity Rule:** Never script or depict the promoted product broken, torn, wobbling, or leaking—compare against generic frustration verbally, not by damaging `@ProductAuthority`.
