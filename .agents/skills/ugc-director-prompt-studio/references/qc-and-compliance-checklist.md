# Pre-Flight QC & Compliance Checklist

Run this scorecard on every script + Storyboard/Flow prompt package before rendering or exporting.

---

## 1. Scriptwriter & Hook Retention Audit
- [ ] **3-Second Hook & Emotional Pain Trigger:** Scene 1 opens by touching a real, relatable frustration (*Painful Nyata*) or visual proof—zero TV clichés (*"Halo guys..."*).
- [ ] **Clear Problem Solved (Added Value):** Clearly explains what exact problem the product solves and why it beats generic options rather than reading a dry title.
- [ ] **Safe Before vs After Contrast:** Highlights tangible everyday Before-vs-After transformation cleanly without banned absolute medical claims and without showing `@ProductAuthority` damaged.
- [ ] **Natural Spoken Indonesian (`12–16` Words):** Every `vo_script_id` reads aloud smoothly in conversational Indonesian without stiff translation phrasing.
- [ ] **Verified Facts Only:** Specs, dimensions, materials, and benefits match grounded user specs/images—zero invented numbers or fake promos.
- [ ] **Objection Answered + CTA:** At least one real buyer doubt is resolved in-script (plus `pinned_comment_faq_id`) leading into a clear platform CTA.
- [ ] **CapCut Headline Concise:** Every `on_screen_headline_id` is `<= 5` impactful words.

## 2. UGC Creator & Visual Authenticity Audit
- [ ] **Indonesian Context Locked:** Environment feels relatable (Indonesian sunlit living room, tidy workspace, clean bathroom vanity, outdoor patio)—never sterile Western stock rooms.
- [ ] **Avatar Consistency:** Creator mode uses standalone space-bounded `@me` paired with Indonesian cue; Faceless mode omits human faces cleanly.
- [ ] **Audio-Visual Sync:** Scene pacing balances medium shots, macro detail cuts, and hero framing rather than repeating the exact same framing 5 times.
- [ ] **Zero Defective Product Visuals:** Promoted product is never shown broken, torn, leaking, wobbling, or malfunctioning.

## 3. Google Flow (Veo 3.1) Anti-Halu Prompt Audit
- [ ] **Word Budget Strict:** Every `flow_prompt_en` is `<= 35` words total (target `< 30` core + suffix).
- [ ] **Reference Tagging:** `@ProductAuthority` appears cleanly in every prompt (plus `@me` where creator is on camera).
- [ ] **Zero Re-Descriptions:** Prompt never repeats bottle/chair/bag colors, cap shapes, leg geometry, or printed logos already locked in `@ProductAuthority`.
- [ ] **Zero Banned Verbs:** No `walks toward`, `picks up`, `opens`, `grabs`, `unpacks`, `twists`, `rotates`, `bends`, `folds`, `unfolds`, `collapses`, `morphs`.
- [ ] **Clean Syntax & Negative Suffix:** Zero `[...]` brackets, zero `"` quotes, exact suffix `No text overlays, no additional logos, no readable screens.`, and silent/natural foley audio cue (no unwanted AI voice).
- [ ] **Valid Scene Builder Sequence:** Scene 1 = `New Scene`; `Extend` starts with `Seamless continuation from the previous frame,`; `Jump to` re-tags `@ProductAuthority`.

## 4. Shopee Video & TikTok Shop Compliance Audit
- [ ] **Exact CTA Alignment:**
  - Shopee Video -> `Keranjang Oranye` / `Tautan Produk di bawah` (zero `Keranjang Kuning`).
  - TikTok Shop -> `Keranjang Kuning`.
  - Dual (`BOTH`) -> neutral `cek keranjang di bawah` in VO, separate platform captions.
- [ ] **Zero Policy Violations:** No absolute medical cure promises, no fake cheapest-in-Indonesia claims, and no WhatsApp/DM/off-platform checkout redirects.
